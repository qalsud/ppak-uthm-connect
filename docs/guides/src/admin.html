People & registrations

Keep the right people in the right class and centre.

Review a registration

  1. Open Registrations.
  2. Filter by Pending.
  3. Open the applicant, check the details, then approve or reject.
  4. Approving creates an active parent account.

Add a teacher

  1. Open Teachers → Add teacher.
  2. Enter the name, email and a temporary password.
  3. Choose a class and at least one centre.
  4. Save. The teacher now sees only that class and centre.

Add a child

  1. Open Students → Add student.
  2. Class and centre are required.
  3. Link the parent account and add allergies or medical notes.
  4. Save. The child appears in the teacher’s register.

Find an account

  • Use the status filter for Pending, Active, Awaiting, Inactive or Rejected.
  • Search by name or email.
  • Use Deactivate instead of deleting an account.
  • Deleted records can be restored from Recently deleted.

Daily operations

The same screens teachers use, available to you from the admin menu.

Teacher views

  • Attendance — check children in, out or absent.
  • Daily activities — meals, sleep, medication and notes.
  • Progress — ratings, notes and photos.
  • Growth — height, weight and BMI.
  • Daily updates and Messages.

Attendance rules

  • Select the class and date first.
  • Future days are view-only.
  • Mark absent records a reason without a parent request.
  • An approved absence appears as Absent automatically.
  • Check-ins and check-outs are recorded in the activity log.

Absences & medication

  • Parent absence requests appear in the attendance screen.
  • Approve or decline with an optional note.
  • Approval is blocked if the child already attended that day.
  • Medication requests are reviewed from the same screen.

What parents see

Parents see attendance, daily updates, activities, progress, growth, fees and memos for their own children only. Anything you record here reaches them immediately.

  • They cannot see another family’s child.
  • They cannot mark attendance; only the centre can.

Fees & communication

Close the month, send the receipt, and keep the conversation moving.

Generate monthly fees

  1. Choose the active centre in the top bar.
  2. Open Payments → Generate.
  3. Pick the month and confirm.
  4. Review the records; mark one paid or unpaid.

Receipts & online payments

  • Download a receipt from any paid record.
  • Parents can download the same receipt from their Financials page.
  • Online card payments are disabled in this demo; Stripe is not configured.
  • Use Mark paid for the demo.

Memos

  • Target everyone, parents, teachers or one class.
  • A class memo needs the class and is limited to that centre.
  • Attach up to three photos.
  • Memos appear in the parent and teacher Memos pages.

Messages

  • Open Conversations for an overview of every thread.
  • Open Teacher views → Messages for the inbox layout.
  • Reply as the centre; unread badges update automatically.
  • Admins can review and moderate conversations.
Try this in the demo Generate fees for one centre, mark one record paid, then download its receipt. Point out that the other centre did not receive any records.

Your 10-minute demo

A calm, predictable path through the system.

  1. Dashboard & centre switch. Show the overview and pick the active centre.Admin dashboard → centre selector
  2. Approve a registration. Show the parent account being created.Registrations → Pending
  3. Prove teacher scope. Open the teacher list and show class + centre.Teachers → Add teacher
  4. Attendance. Check in one child; mark another absent.Teacher views → Attendance
  5. Parent communication. Open a thread and reply.Teacher views → Messages
  6. Fees. Generate for the active centre, mark paid, download a receipt.Payments
  7. Announcement. Publish a class memo.Memos → Add memo

Keep it safe

  • Do not run Reseed demo during the presentation.
  • Do not show or share passwords on screen.
  • Use the active-centre switcher before fee generation.
  • Stripe and online card payments are intentionally off.

If something looks wrong

  • Check the active centre first.
  • Refresh; the notification and chat badges poll in the background.
  • Use Recently deleted to restore a removed record.
  • Activity log shows who changed what.
