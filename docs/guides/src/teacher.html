Attendance

The register is the first thing parents check. Keep it accurate and simple.

Mark the day

  1. Open Attendance.
  2. Choose the class and the date.
  3. Tap At school when the child arrives.
  4. Tap Back home at pickup.

Mark absent

  1. Find the child who is away.
  2. Tap Mark absent.
  3. Choose sick, personal or other.
  4. Add a short note if it helps the family.

Approved absences

  • A parent request approved by the centre appears as Absent automatically.
  • The reason is visible to you and the parent.
  • If the child turns up, check them in; the approved absence is cleared for that day.
  • You cannot check in on a future day unless you are correcting an approved absence.

Checkout details

  • Choose the authorised collector.
  • Add a checkout photo if the setting requires one.
  • Use the override reason only when necessary.
  • Every check-in and check-out is recorded in the activity log.

Activities, updates & growth

Small records make a big difference to a parent’s day.

Daily activities

  1. Open Daily activities.
  2. Choose the child and today’s date.
  3. Record sleep, meals, shower, medication and notes.
  4. Save. The record appears in the parent’s Activities page.

Daily updates

  1. Open Daily updates.
  2. Choose the child and write a short note.
  3. Add a photo if you have one.
  4. Send. The parent sees it in their Update page and can reply.

Growth

  • Record height and weight for the month.
  • BMI is calculated automatically.
  • Parents see the growth history and chart.
  • Re-recording the same month updates the existing entry.

Keep it useful

Use everyday language, not codes. One clear sentence and one photo is often more reassuring than a long report.

Progress, memos & messages

Show the learning, share the news, and answer the question.

Record progress

  1. Open Progress and choose the child.
  2. Pick the date and the activity.
  3. Rate activity performance, skill mastery and development area.
  4. Add a note or photo, then save.

Fix a record

  • Your recent progress records are listed below the form.
  • Use the pencil to load a record back into the form.
  • Use the bin to delete a record you created.
  • Admins can also correct or remove a record.

Publish a memo

  1. Open Memos.
  2. Tap Publish memo.
  3. Write the title and message; add up to three photos.
  4. Send. Only parents of your class and centre receive it.

Chat with parents

  • Open Messages and choose a child.
  • Send text or a photo.
  • The unread badge shows new replies.
  • Keep conversations about the child; use the memo for whole-class news.

Your 8-minute demo

A simple path that shows the whole teacher day.

  1. Dashboard. Point out your class, today’s activities and updates.Teacher dashboard
  2. Attendance. Check a child in, then mark another absent.Attendance → select class and date
  3. Daily activity. Record sleep or a meal for one child.Daily activities
  4. Daily update. Send a note with a photo.Daily updates
  5. Progress. Record one rating and note.Progress → choose child
  6. Communication. Publish a memo, then reply in Messages.Memos / Messages

Good to know

  • You only see your assigned class and centre.
  • Parents see attendance, activities, updates, progress and memos.
  • There is no numeric gradebook; the progress ratings are the record.
  • Photos are watermarked automatically.

If something looks wrong

  • Check the selected class and date first.
  • Refresh once if a badge or chat looks stale.
  • Future attendance is intentionally read-only.
  • Absence approval is done by the centre, not the teacher.
