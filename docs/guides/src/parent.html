Attendance & daily updates

Know where your child is and what happened today.

Check attendance

  1. Open Attendance.
  2. Find your child’s card.
  3. Read today’s status.
  4. Review the history list underneath.

What the status means

  • At school — checked in by the teacher.
  • Back home — checked out at pickup.
  • Absent — marked by the teacher or an approved request.
  • Not arrived yet — no attendance recorded.

Read the daily update

  1. Open Daily update.
  2. Read the teacher’s note and photo.
  3. Add your own note or photo for the teacher.
  4. Send it; the teacher is notified.

See the day’s activities

  • Open Activities.
  • See meals, sleep, shower, medication and notes.
  • Records are entered by the teacher.
  • You cannot edit a teacher’s activity record.
Try this in the demo Open attendance for one child, then read the latest daily update and activities.

Your child’s profile

Progress, growth and the details the centre needs.

Open a child

  1. From the dashboard, tap a child card.
  2. Review the profile, health and medical details.
  3. Scroll to progress, growth and attendance.
  4. Use Messages to ask the teacher about anything unclear.

Read progress

  • Activity performance — how the child did today.
  • Skill mastery — how well the skill was practised.
  • Development area — the domain the activity targets.
  • Teacher notes and photos appear with the record.

Follow growth

  • Height, weight and BMI are recorded by the teacher.
  • The history shows changes over time.
  • Use it as a conversation starter, not a diagnosis.
  • Ask the teacher if a measurement looks wrong.

Medical details

Allergies and medical notes are shown to the teacher and the centre. Keep them up to date and tell the teacher about any new allergy or medication.

Fees & receipts

See what is due, what is paid, and keep the receipt.

Check your balance

  1. Open Financials.
  2. Find your child’s card.
  3. Review unpaid and paid months.
  4. Overdue months are highlighted.

Pay in the demo

  • Online card payments are disabled because Stripe is not configured.
  • The centre can mark a record paid manually.
  • Once paid, the status changes to Paid.
  • Refresh the page if the status was changed by the admin.

Download a receipt

  1. Open Financials.
  2. Find your child and a month marked Paid.
  3. Tap the download icon on that row.
  4. The receipt PDF opens for saving or printing.

Remember

A receipt is only available for a paid record. If a month is unpaid, ask the centre to confirm the payment first.

Messages, memos & absences

Stay in touch and tell the centre when your child will be away.

Message the teacher

  1. Open Messages.
  2. Choose the child’s conversation.
  3. Send a message or a photo.
  4. Unread replies show a badge in the header.

Read memos

  • Open Memos for centre announcements.
  • Some memos are for all parents; some are only for your child’s class.
  • Photos from the centre appear inside the memo.
  • Memos are read-only for parents.

Report an absence

  1. Open Attendance.
  2. Find your child’s card and tap Report absence.
  3. Pick today or a future date; add a reason.
  4. Attach a document if the centre asks for one.

Absence rules

  • Past dates cannot be requested.
  • If the child already attended that day, the request is blocked.
  • The centre approves or declines; you are notified.
  • An approved absence appears as Absent in attendance.
Try this in the demo Open Messages and send one short reply, then show the class memo and the absence request form.
